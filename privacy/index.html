YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, plainly.

What this preview does with information, and what needs to be in place before Pester AI starts sending texts.

Early-preview policy · Updated October 18, 2026

Who operates this site

Pester AI is a Pajak Enterprises project operated by Franciszek Pajak as a sole proprietor. Privacy questions can be sent to franciszek@pajakenterprises.com.

What happens in the preview today

The signup preview uses a fictional phone number for the SMS recipient. Its selected role, sample schedule, warning preference, and checkbox states are used only to update the page in your browser. This site code does not transmit them, store them in cookies or local storage, or create an account or SMS subscription. It does not request notification permission or send notifications.

The preview does not monitor your device, apps, browsing history, or focus. It includes no advertising or analytics integrations and loads no third-party fonts. No preview form data is sent to Bird, Convex, or an AI provider.

The server hosting the website necessarily receives connection information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time. Its configured access and error logs may record that information for operation, troubleshooting, and security.

If you contact Franciszek

If you email for help, the information you choose to provide, such as your contact details and the content of your request, is used to respond and address the issue. Email service providers handle the communication. Please do not include passwords or private information about your friends.

Public program status

When a Convex deployment is configured, the product page requests public program availability from Convex Cloud. That request does not include a phone number, signup choices, focus activity, or a consent record. Like the website host, Convex receives connection information such as the requester's IP address to handle the request. The preview does not create personal records in the Convex database.

What the future service would need

Before enrollment opens, this policy will be updated to describe the actual implementation. The planned program would need the participants' account identities, the SMS recipient's verified phone number, shared accountability settings, consent and withdrawal records, and the agreed focus events used to trigger alerts. The person focusing does not need to supply a phone number for reminder texts in this flow.

If the person focusing enables push notifications, the service would also need a device or browser push subscription and the relevant permission and preference records. These are separate from SMS consent. The actual notification providers and data handling will be described before push notifications launch.

Consent evidence would include when and how permission was given and which wording and agreement version the person accepted. Message content and delivery information would be used to operate the program, investigate problems, and honor requests to stop. Only focus details explicitly agreed to by the participants should be shared with their chosen partner.

The future service plans to use Bird to deliver texts and managed Convex Cloud to store application records. A model provider may be used for specifically disclosed AI features. Bird messaging, personal-data storage, and AI processing are not active in this preview. Any future AI processing and device permissions must be explained before affected information is collected or used.

Sharing and SMS consent

Phone numbers and SMS opt-in records will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are excluded from any marketing-data sharing.

When the service launches, providers such as Bird may process the information necessary to deliver and support the messaging service under the operator's instructions. A focus-information sharing agreement between two users does not authorize selling either person's contact details or allowing another business to market to them.

Retention and deletion

The preview creates no stored signup or consent record. Contact correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Hosting-log retention depends on the server configuration and must be verified when this preview is deployed.

Before the live service opens, the operator will set and disclose retention periods for account information, focus events, message records, backups, and consent evidence. Withdrawal must stop future program messages; a limited opt-out record may need to remain to prevent re-enrollment or accidental sends.

Contact Franciszek to ask what information is held about you or to request access, correction, or deletion. Requests will be handled as applicable, with identity verification where needed and an explanation if some records must be retained.

Your choices

You can use this public site without an account. The preview enrolls nobody. In the planned live program, either participant may end the shared agreement, and recipients may reply STOP to withdraw from program texts. Email support will also be available. See the SMS Terms for the proposed program rules.
