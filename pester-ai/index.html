PESTER AI · A PAJAK ENTERPRISES PRODUCT

Keep the promise.
Bring the backup.

For students, independent workers, and anyone who wants a little help following through. Pester AI is being built to text your consenting accountability partner when you miss a check-in you both agreed on.

This is an early preview. Enrollment is not open. No phone numbers are collected through this preview and no texts are sent.

Your focus.
Your shared rules.

Say you want to spend 45 minutes writing instead of scrolling through reels. You agree with a friend on a check-in. If you miss it, the planned service can let that friend know, using only the details you agreed to share.

01 / THE AGREEMENT

Choose the boundaries.

Agree on the goal, trigger, message window, daily SMS limit, and focus information that can be shared. Choose whether you want an advance notification and an agreed chance to refocus before the partner alert.

02 / THE ACCEPTANCE

Invite them yourself.

In the planned flow, you share an invitation link personally. Your friend must accept and opt in themselves before Pester AI can text them.

03 / THE FOLLOW-THROUGH

Stick to the agreement.

Program texts stay within the agreed limits. A changed agreement needs both people to accept it again. Either person can withdraw.

GOOD QUESTIONS

The details matter.

Who actually sends the messages?

Pester AI would send automated texts on behalf of the agreed accountability program. A friend's participation does not replace your own SMS consent. These are service messages, not a friend manually texting you.

Do I have to receive texts while I'm focusing?

No. The proposed flow sends SMS to your consenting partner. You can choose an advance notification to yourself, or no advance notification. Notification permission is separate from SMS consent. Any warning period and what happens when a notification cannot be delivered need to be agreed before launch.

Can you see my apps or browsing activity?

This preview does not monitor your device, browser tabs, Instagram, or focus activity. Focus detection is not implemented. Any future monitoring would need a defined mechanism, clear disclosure, and your permission before it starts.

How often would the partner get a text?

The two participants set the SMS frequency, daily maximum, and timing in their agreement. Frequency varies with those settings and the agreed focus events. Enrollment confirmations and replies to STOP or HELP are additional service messages to the SMS recipient. The person focusing receives no program SMS in this flow.

What happens if one person changes their mind?

The planned behavior is to end the shared agreement and stop its notifications and partner alerts. Either participant would be able to end it in the app or through support; the SMS recipient could also reply STOP. Restarting would require fresh acceptance from both participants.

Is Pester AI free?

There is no Pester AI subscription or per-message service charge planned for this initial program. Your mobile carrier's message and data rates may still apply. The preview itself sends no texts.
