ACCOUNTABILITY, BY AGREEMENT

Make a plan.
Bring a friend.

The idea is simple: choose a focus goal and agree when Pester AI may text your consenting partner. You can choose an advance notification to yourself, or let the agreed partner alert go ahead without one.

01 /

Decide what matters.

Choose a focus session, the check-ins, and what your partner is allowed to hear about.

02 /

Both say yes.

Each person accepts the same agreement. The friend receiving texts gives their own SMS permission. Nobody can opt in on a friend's behalf.

03 /

A chance to lock in.

Choose an optional advance notification before Pester AI texts your partner. You do not need reminder texts yourself. If either person leaves, the agreement ends for both.
