CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

SMS terms.

How the planned Pester AI accountability messaging program would work.

Draft for the early preview · Updated October 18, 2026

This is an early preview. Enrollment is not open. No phone numbers are collected through this preview and no texts are sent.

1. Program and operator

Pester AI (styled Pester.ai) is being built and operated by Franciszek Pajak, a sole proprietor using the Pajak Enterprises name. These terms describe the proposed US SMS program. This website currently demonstrates the planned flow; it does not activate agreements or SMS subscriptions.

2. What messages are for

The planned SMS program sends automated accountability alerts to friends who independently agree to receive them. It may also send enrollment confirmations, agreement-status messages, and replies to requests for help or cancellation to those SMS recipients. SMS permission for this program does not include promotional offers.

The person focusing would receive no program SMS in this flow. They may choose an optional advance notification before a partner alert, subject to separate device permission, or choose no advance notification. Notifications and their delivery are not implemented in this preview.

SMS messages identify Pester AI as the sender. A personal accountability agreement does not make an automated message a manually sent message from your friend.

3. Both people must choose to participate

Before activation, both participants must accept the agreement and the focus information to be shared. The SMS recipient must also verify their own number and separately accept the SMS disclosures. One person cannot consent on behalf of another. An invitation alone does not enroll its recipient.

SMS consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchase. Notification permission does not grant SMS consent, and the person focusing does not need an SMS subscription. These are participation rules for the service, not a claim that Pester AI legally enforces private contracts.

4. Frequency, timing, and changes

SMS frequency varies according to the participants' shared settings and agreed focus events. Before enrollment, the agreement must show a daily program-text limit for the partner, sending window, and time zone. Both people must accept those settings. Enrollment confirmations and STOP/HELP replies are additional service messages to the SMS recipient.

The options on the preview are examples. No active frequency or schedule exists yet. Any advance-warning period and behavior when a notification cannot be delivered must also be defined before launch. Changes to an active agreement would require renewed acceptance from both people before the changed rules are used.

5. Cost and delivery

Pester AI's initial program is planned to be free, with no subscription or per-message service charge. Message and data rates may apply under your mobile plan. Ask your carrier about your plan's charges.

SMS delivery can be delayed or fail. This is an accountability aid, not an emergency or safety monitoring service. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

6. Stopping ends the agreement

Once live, the SMS recipient can reply STOP to a Pester AI program message to withdraw from program texts. They may receive an opt-out confirmation. Either participant would also be able to end the agreement in the app or ask the operator using the contact details below.

If either participant withdraws, the shared agreement ends and no new notifications or partner alerts for that agreement should be sent. A message already handed to a carrier might still arrive. A future restart requires a new agreement accepted by both participants; a START reply alone must not reactivate the old agreement. Changing a device's notification permission alone does not communicate withdrawal from the agreement; use the app or support to end it.

7. Help and support

Once the program is active, reply HELP to a program message for support details. For help now, email franciszek@pajakenterprises.com. Franciszek handles support for Pajak Enterprises and Pester AI.

8. Privacy and launch

Read the Privacy Policy for current preview data practices and the planned handling of SMS information. The final enrollment flow, sending number, data practices, and these terms must be confirmed before subscriptions open. Nobody is subscribed by using this preview.
